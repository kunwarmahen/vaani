Pucks

Last questions

Each question, step by step: what woke the puck, what Whisper heard, whose question it was taken to be, how long your agent took, and whether the puck came for the answer. A step that stopped is red. This page looks again every few seconds.

    Voices

    So a puck can tell who's asking. A voice is learned at the puck itself: press Learn their voice, then that person says the wake word and any sentence there, three times. Only a few numbers about the voice are kept, never the sound. Which people a puck tells apart is a setting (with Sarathi: Settings → Vaani).

    PINs

    Some things your agent does need more than a yes: buying, sending, or changing something after it read a web page or an email. At the puck, those ask for the person's PIN, said one digit at a time. A PIN is kept only as a hash, never the digits, and never goes to your agent. Two wrong tries and the question goes to the phone; five wrong in an hour lock it at the puck for the hour.
